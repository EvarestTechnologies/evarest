## 1. Company overview

Evarest Technologies Ltd is a Kenya-based technology company that builds its own technology products and provides software engineering services to other organisations.

The company has two connected business activities.

1. **Product development.** Building and developing ZEYA and Kintaraa to address real-world problems.
2. **Engineering services.** Helping clients build, integrate, deploy, maintain, and improve software systems.

Revenue from engineering services is intended to support the development and growth of Evarest's own products. Funding, research collaborations, implementation partnerships, and other appropriate opportunities may also support product development.

Evarest aims to grow a sustainable engineering business while building products that can serve users and organisations in multiple markets.

## 2. Origin story

Evarest grew out of approximately two years of software development training at Zone01. During that period, the founders participated in internal and external hackathons, working together to develop solutions to real-world problems. Some of their projects won competitions.

Kintaraa, developed in response to concerns about femicide and gender-based violence, was one of these projects.

After their training, the founders considered their next steps. They needed employment opportunities but also saw the potential to build a company together, create work, and develop solutions to meaningful problems.

Evarest emerged from that decision. It combines the founders' engineering capabilities, its own product ambitions, and the opportunity to serve other organisations through software development.

## 7. Products

ZEYA and Kintaraa are Evarest's two in-house products. They should receive equal prominence on the website.

### ZEYA

**Purpose:** A WhatsApp-based pregnancy education companion for women in Kenya.

ZEYA is intended to make pregnancy education more accessible through conversational interactions. Its content draws on established health guidance, including World Health Organization and Kenya Ministry of Health guidance.

**Current status:** Preparing for a research trial. It is not yet open to the public.

**Platform:** Built using Open Chat Studio, which Evarest self-hosts.

**Safety position:** ZEYA provides educational information. It is not a diagnostic service and does not replace a qualified health worker. The product includes an emergency-message pathway for pregnancy danger signs that operates before AI processing.

**Potential partners:** Research institutions, digital health organisations, implementers, and funders aligned with maternal health education.

**Website call to action:** Discuss research and implementation partnerships.

### Kintaraa

**Purpose:** An anonymity-first platform for survivors of gender-based violence.

Kintaraa was developed in response to concerns about femicide and gender-based violence. It forms part of Evarest's work on technology intended to address real-world social challenges.

**Current status:** Deployed and awaiting its planned Nairobi County pilot. It is not yet open to the public.

**Potential partners:** Relevant community organisations, public-sector implementers, research collaborators, and funders.

**Website call to action:** Discuss implementation and strategic partnerships.

### Product communication principles

The website must distinguish clearly between development, deployment, pilot testing, research, and public availability.

It must not imply that either product has achieved measured impact, adoption at scale, or independently validated outcomes unless supporting evidence is available.

Any public description of sensitive product features, research plans, users, or implementation arrangements must be reviewed for privacy, safety, and partner permissions.
