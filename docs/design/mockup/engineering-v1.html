Questions

Before you get in touch.

Do you work with teams outside Kenya?

Yes. We work with clients in Kenya and in other markets, and we agree how we communicate and report progress at the start.

Can you work alongside our own engineers?

Yes. We can add capacity to your team, or take full ownership of an agreed piece of work.

What happens after launch?

You get documentation and a handover. If you want us to keep going, a monthly retainer covers maintenance, fixes and new features.

How do we start?

Email us with what you are building and when you need it. We reply, set up a first call, and send a proposal once we understand the work.

Have something that needs to be built?

Tell us what you are building and when you need it. We reply by email.
