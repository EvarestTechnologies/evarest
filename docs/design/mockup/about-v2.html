Our story

Most teams meet when the project starts. We’d already been building together for two years.

We trained as software engineers side by side and entered hackathons as a team, where the software has to work by the deadline. Some of those projects won. One of them, Kintaraa, mattered too much to leave as a demo, so we kept building it.

When you work with Evarest, you get a team that already knows how to ship together. And because we run products of our own, we know launch day is where the work starts, not where it ends.
