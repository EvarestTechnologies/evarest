ZEYA

A pregnancy education companion on WhatsApp for women in Kenya, in English and Kiswahili. Each answer fits her week of pregnancy.

  • ✓
    Grounded in health guidanceAnswers draw on World Health Organization and Kenya Ministry of Health guidance.
  • ✓
    Danger signs come firstEight danger signs, such as heavy bleeding, get emergency numbers at once, before any AI runs.
  • ✓
    Where she already isNo app to install. She messages ZEYA on WhatsApp.
Her message→Danger-sign check→Emergency numbers, or an answer for her week
Chat with ZEYA →More about ZEYA

ZEYA provides education, not diagnosis. It does not replace a health worker.

ZEYA

Privacy and research

Consent first

ZEYA asks for consent before storing messages. Anyone can say no.

Open to everyone in Kenya

Only women enrolled by the study team take part in the research study. Other users are not included in it.

Study numbers, not names

Researchers analyse study conversations under a study number instead of a name or phone number.

Leaving the study

Participants can withdraw at any time by contacting the study team. It does not affect their care.
